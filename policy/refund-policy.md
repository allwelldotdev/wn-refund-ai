# Refund Policy

Every request is checked against all of the rules below. When more than one rule applies, the most restrictive outcome wins: a denial outranks a review, and a review outranks an approval.

1. **Final sale items are not refundable.** Items marked as final sale at purchase cannot be refunded.
2. **Refund window.** Refunds must be requested within 30 days of delivery (or of the order date if no delivery is recorded). Later requests are denied.
3. **Human review for large refunds.** Refunds above $500.00 are reviewed by a support agent before any approval.
4. **Damaged or incorrect items.** Items that arrived damaged, or that differ from what was ordered, are eligible for a refund.
5. **Repeat claims.** Customers with 2 or more refund claims in the last 180 days have further claims reviewed by a support agent.
6. **Conflicting claims.** Requests that conflict with our order records, or that contradict earlier statements, are reviewed by a support agent.

Any request not covered by an applicable rule is reviewed by a support agent.
