A

Greeting + order chips

Recent orders as chips. Orders with an approved, denied or in-progress request are marked with that status, can't be picked, and link to the request.

B

Composer near the limit

Counter turns bold at 450 characters, red when over 500. Send is disabled when over.

C

Reviewing

Spinner, elapsed time and three named steps that tick over, so it never looks stuck. Steps still update with reduced motion.

D

Verdict — approved

After streaming: explanation, amount, refund method, line items, reference. The composer is replaced by a black “Start new request” button; the chat is saved in Your requests.

E

Verdict — denied

Plain-language reason and the policy it came from. The request closes; if disputes are on, the card points to Your requests to dispute it.

F

Verdict — escalated

Who handles it, the expected reply time, and where the reply will arrive.

G

Automatic review failed

No error shown. A normal Escalated card says a specialist will review. Internal retries stay invisible.

H

Rate limit

Neutral notice with a countdown; the composer is paused, not cleared.

I

Session expired

Sign in again goes to the sign-in page; the unsent message is kept.

J

No orders

Empty state for a new customer; free text still goes to a specialist.

K

Your requests

Past requests with status badges and reference IDs.

L

Verdict — streaming

Badge and reference appear first, so the outcome is clear. The explanation streams in with a cursor; details follow when it ends.

M

Your requests — denied, can dispute

Read-only chat for an automatic denial. No composer; “Dispute this decision” asks for confirmation, then moves it to Escalations.

N

Your requests — denied after review

A specialist made this decision, so it is final. No dispute option is shown.

O

Your requests — disputes turned off

Same automatic denial with the admin setting off: the decision is final and no dispute option is shown.
